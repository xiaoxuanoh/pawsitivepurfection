Our aim

To bring pets their products from all over the world. Why?

  1. Chinese exclusives (smart feeders, thermal jackets, budget smart litter boxes) can’t be shipped out;
  2. Overseas-only special diets, wild Alaskan salmon oil or EU-approved dewormers never reach Chinese living rooms;
  3. Add busy schedules + scary fake-goods stories → cross-border shopping is stuck.
